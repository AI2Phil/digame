# Getting Started with Digame

Welcome to Digame! This guide will help you get up and running with your Digital Professional Twin Platform in just a few minutes.

## 🎯 What You'll Accomplish

By the end of this guide, you'll have:
- ✅ Created and verified your account
- ✅ Completed the interactive onboarding process
- ✅ Set up your profile and preferences
- ✅ Connected your first integration
- ✅ Explored your productivity dashboard
- ✅ Downloaded the mobile app

**Estimated Time**: 15-20 minutes

## 🚀 Step 1: Account Creation

### Sign Up Process
1. **Visit the Registration Page**
   - Go to [https://app.digame.ai/register](https://app.digame.ai/register)
   - Or click "Get Started" from the homepage

2. **Enter Your Information**
   ```
   Email: your.email@company.com
   Password: [Strong password with 8+ characters]
   Full Name: Your Full Name
   Company: Your Organization (optional)
   ```

3. **Verify Your Email**
   - Check your email for a verification link
   - Click the link to activate your account
   - Return to the login page

### First Login
1. **Sign In**
   - Go to [https://app.digame.ai/login](https://app.digame.ai/login)
   - Enter your email and password
   - Click "Sign In"

2. **Security Setup** (Recommended)
   - Enable two-factor authentication for enhanced security
   - Add a recovery email if prompted

## 🔗 Step 4: Connect Your First Integration

Integrations help Digame understand your work patterns and provide better insights.

### Recommended First Integrations

#### Calendar Integration (Highly Recommended)
1. **Google Calendar**
   - Click "Integrations" in the sidebar
   - Select "Google Calendar"
   - Click "Connect" and authorize access
   - Choose calendars to sync

2. **Microsoft Outlook**
   - Select "Microsoft Outlook"
   - Sign in with your Microsoft account
   - Grant necessary permissions

**Benefits:**
- Automatic meeting time tracking
- Focus time identification
- Schedule optimization suggestions

#### Email Integration
1. **Gmail**
   - Navigate to Integrations
   - Select "Gmail"
   - Authorize access (read-only)

2. **Outlook Email**
   - Choose "Outlook Email"
   - Sign in and grant permissions

**Benefits:**
- Email pattern analysis
- Response time tracking
- Communication efficiency insights

#### Project Management Tools
1. **Popular Options:**
   - Jira (for development teams)
   - Asana (for project management)
   - Trello (for task organization)
   - Monday.com (for team collaboration)

2. **Setup Process:**
   - Select your tool from the integrations list
   - Follow the OAuth flow
   - Choose projects/boards to sync

**Benefits:**
- Task completion tracking
- Project progress insights
- Workload analysis

### Integration Setup Tips
- **Start Small**: Connect 1-2 integrations initially
- **Check Permissions**: Review what data is accessed
- **Test Sync**: Verify data is flowing correctly
- **Privacy**: Understand data usage and storage

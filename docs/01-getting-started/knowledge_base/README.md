## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### 🚀 Getting Started
**Q: How long does it take to see meaningful insights?**
A: Most users see initial insights within 3-5 days, with comprehensive patterns emerging after 1-2 weeks of consistent use.

**Q: What integrations should I set up first?**
A: Start with your calendar and primary communication tool (email/Slack). These provide the most immediate value for productivity tracking.

**Q: Is my data secure and private?**
A: Yes, Digame uses enterprise-grade security with encryption at rest and in transit. You control your data sharing preferences and can export or delete your data at any time.

### 📊 Analytics & Insights
**Q: How is my productivity score calculated?**
A: The productivity score combines multiple factors including focus time, goal progress, task completion, and meeting efficiency, benchmarked against similar roles and industries.

**Q: Can I customize which metrics are tracked?**
A: Yes, you can configure which activities are tracked, set custom goals, and choose which metrics appear on your dashboard.

**Q: How accurate is the automatic time tracking?**
A: Automatic tracking is typically 85-95% accurate. You can always manually adjust or add activities to improve accuracy.

### 👥 Team Features
**Q: How do team analytics work?**
A: Team analytics aggregate individual data while maintaining privacy. Team members can choose what data to share, and all data is anonymized for team-level insights.

**Q: Can managers see individual employee data?**
A: Only with explicit employee consent. By default, managers see aggregated team metrics and trends, not individual detailed data.

**Q: How do I set up a team?**
A: Team admins can create teams and invite members. Members can choose their participation level and data sharing preferences.

### 🤖 AI Features
**Q: How does AI coaching work?**
A: AI coaching analyzes your work patterns, goals, and industry best practices to provide personalized recommendations for improving productivity and achieving your objectives.

**Q: Can I turn off AI features?**
A: Yes, all AI features are optional and can be disabled in your privacy settings. You can choose which AI services to use and which data to share.

**Q: How does Digame protect my privacy with AI?**
A: AI processing can be done locally or with privacy-preserving techniques. You control what data is used for AI analysis and can opt out at any time.

### 🔧 Technical Questions
**Q: What browsers are supported?**
A: Digame supports all modern browsers including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. We recommend using the latest version for the best experience.

**Q: Is there an offline mode?**
A: The mobile app supports offline functionality, allowing you to log activities and view cached data without an internet connection.

**Q: Can I export my data?**
A: Yes, you can export your data in multiple formats (CSV, JSON, PDF) at any time through the settings page.

### 🏢 Enterprise Questions
**Q: How does multi-tenancy work?**
A: Enterprise customers get isolated tenant environments with separate data, users, and configurations while sharing the same platform infrastructure.

**Q: What compliance standards does Digame meet?**
A: Digame is designed to meet SOC 2, GDPR, HIPAA, and other major compliance standards. Contact our enterprise team for specific compliance documentation.

**Q: Can we integrate with our existing systems?**
A: Yes, Digame offers extensive integration capabilities through APIs, webhooks, and pre-built connectors for popular enterprise tools.

## 📝 Glossary

### Core Concepts

**Digital Professional Twin**
A comprehensive digital representation of your professional activities, patterns, and growth trajectory, powered by data analytics and AI.

**Productivity Score**
A composite metric that measures overall productivity based on focus time, goal achievement, task completion, and other performance indicators.

**Focus Time**
Uninterrupted work periods where you're engaged in deep, concentrated work without meetings or distractions.

**Behavioral Patterns**
Recurring patterns in your work habits, communication style, and productivity rhythms identified through data analysis.

### Features & Components

**Dashboard**
Your main productivity command center displaying key metrics, insights, and recommendations in a customizable interface.

**Workflow Automation**
Automated processes that trigger actions based on specific conditions, helping streamline repetitive tasks and procedures.

**AI Coaching**
Personalized recommendations and guidance provided by artificial intelligence based on your work patterns and goals.

**Integration**
Connection between Digame and external tools (calendar, email, project management) to automatically collect productivity data.

### Analytics Terms

**Benchmarking**
Comparing your performance metrics against industry standards, similar roles, or team averages to provide context for your data.

**Predictive Analytics**
Using historical data and machine learning to forecast future trends, performance, and outcomes.

**Correlation Analysis**
Identifying relationships between different activities or factors that influence your productivity and performance.

**Trend Analysis**
Examining patterns and changes in your data over time to identify improvement opportunities and track progress.

### Team & Collaboration

**Team Analytics**
Aggregated insights about team performance, collaboration patterns, and collective productivity metrics.

**Peer Matching**
AI-powered system that connects you with colleagues who have complementary skills, similar goals, or relevant experience.

**Collaboration Patterns**
Analysis of how team members interact, communicate, and work together, identifying optimization opportunities.

**Knowledge Sharing**
Systematic capture and distribution of expertise, best practices, and lessons learned within teams and organizations.

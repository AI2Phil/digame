## 🚀 Getting Started

### Q: How long does it take to see meaningful insights in Digame?
**A:** Most users see initial insights within 3-5 days of consistent use. Here's the typical timeline:
- **Day 1-2**: Basic activity tracking begins
- **Day 3-5**: Initial patterns start to emerge
- **Week 1**: Preliminary productivity insights available
- **Week 2-3**: Comprehensive patterns and reliable recommendations
- **Month 1+**: Advanced insights and predictive analytics

**Tip**: The more data you provide through integrations and consistent use, the faster you'll see valuable insights.

### Q: What should I do first after signing up?
**A:** Follow this recommended sequence:
1. **Complete the onboarding process** - This personalizes your experience
2. **Set up your profile** - Add goals, skills, and preferences
3. **Connect your calendar** - This provides immediate productivity insights
4. **Add one communication tool** - Email or Slack for comprehensive tracking
5. **Explore your dashboard** - Familiarize yourself with key metrics
6. **Download the mobile app** - Stay connected on the go

### Q: Do I need to manually track my activities?
**A:** No! Digame automatically tracks most activities through integrations. However, you can:
- **Manual logging**: Add activities not captured automatically
- **Corrections**: Adjust automatically tracked data for accuracy
- **Context**: Add notes or categories to provide more context
- **Goals**: Manually update goal progress when needed

### Q: What integrations should I set up first?
**A:** Prioritize these integrations for maximum immediate value:

**Essential (Week 1):**
- **Calendar** (Google Calendar/Outlook) - Meeting and focus time tracking
- **Email** (Gmail/Outlook) - Communication pattern analysis

**High Value (Week 2):**
- **Communication** (Slack/Teams) - Team collaboration insights
- **Project Management** (Jira/Asana/Trello) - Task and project tracking

**Additional (Month 1):**
- **Time Tracking** (Toggl/Harvest) - Detailed time analysis
- **Development Tools** (GitHub/GitLab) - For developers
- **CRM** (Salesforce/HubSpot) - For sales professionals

## 👤 Account & Profile

### Q: How do I update my profile information?
**A:** To update your profile:
1. Click your profile picture in the top right corner
2. Select "Profile Settings"
3. Edit any section (Basic Info, Skills, Goals, Preferences)
4. Click "Save Changes"

**Note**: Some changes (like role or department) may affect your benchmarking data.

### Q: Can I change my email address?
**A:** Yes, but the process varies by account type:
- **Individual accounts**: Update in Profile Settings → Account → Email
- **Enterprise accounts**: Contact your admin or our support team
- **Verification required**: You'll need to verify the new email address

### Q: How do I delete my account?
**A:** To delete your account:
1. Go to Profile Settings → Account → Privacy
2. Scroll to "Account Deletion"
3. Click "Delete Account"
4. Confirm by typing "DELETE" and your password
5. Your data will be permanently removed within 30 days

**Important**: This action cannot be undone. Consider exporting your data first.

### Q: Can I have multiple accounts?
**A:** Generally, one account per person is recommended for accurate insights. However:
- **Work vs. Personal**: You can have separate accounts for different contexts
- **Multiple Organizations**: Enterprise users may have accounts in different tenants
- **Data Separation**: Each account maintains separate data and insights

## 📊 Analytics & Insights

### Q: How is my productivity score calculated?
**A:** Your productivity score is a composite metric based on:

**Core Factors (70%):**
- **Focus Time Quality** (25%) - Deep work sessions and concentration
- **Goal Progress** (20%) - Advancement toward your objectives
- **Task Completion** (15%) - Efficiency in completing tasks
- **Time Management** (10%) - Effective use of available time

**Contextual Factors (30%):**
- **Meeting Efficiency** (10%) - Quality and necessity of meetings
- **Communication Patterns** (10%) - Effective collaboration
- **Work-Life Balance** (5%) - Sustainable work patterns
- **Learning & Growth** (5%) - Skill development activities

**Benchmarking**: Scores are normalized against similar roles, experience levels, and industries.

### Q: Why does my productivity score fluctuate?
**A:** Productivity scores naturally fluctuate due to:
- **Work Cycles**: Different types of work (creative vs. administrative)
- **Project Phases**: Planning vs. execution vs. review phases
- **External Factors**: Meetings, interruptions, urgent requests
- **Personal Factors**: Energy levels, health, life events
- **Seasonal Patterns**: End of quarter, holidays, vacation periods

**Normal Range**: Most users see 10-20% variation week to week.

### Q: How accurate is the automatic time tracking?
**A:** Automatic tracking accuracy varies by data source:
- **Calendar Events**: 95-99% accurate
- **Email Activity**: 85-95% accurate
- **Application Usage**: 80-90% accurate (when available)
- **Project Management Tools**: 90-95% accurate

**Improving Accuracy**:
- Connect multiple integrations for cross-validation
- Review and correct data weekly
- Add manual entries for offline work
- Use consistent naming conventions

### Q: Can I customize which metrics are displayed?
**A:** Yes! You can customize your dashboard extensively:
- **Widget Selection**: Choose which metrics to display
- **Layout**: Drag and drop widgets to rearrange
- **Time Ranges**: Set default time periods for each widget
- **Filters**: Apply filters to focus on specific data
- **Goals**: Set custom targets and thresholds

### Q: What does "focus time" mean and how is it calculated?
**A:** Focus time represents uninterrupted work periods where you're engaged in deep, concentrated work.

**Calculation Method**:
- **Calendar Analysis**: Blocks of time without meetings
- **Activity Detection**: Periods of consistent application usage
- **Communication Gaps**: Times without email/message responses
- **Manual Logging**: User-reported focus sessions

**Quality Factors**:
- **Duration**: Longer sessions (45+ minutes) score higher
- **Consistency**: Regular focus patterns are valued
- **Depth**: Complex tasks score higher than routine work
- **Outcomes**: Focus time that leads to goal progress

**Minimum Threshold**: 25 minutes to qualify as a focus session.

### Q: How do I set up an integration?
**A:** Integration setup is straightforward:

1. **Navigate to Integrations**:
   - Go to Settings → Integrations
   - Or use the "Connect" button on your dashboard

2. **Choose Your Tool**:
   - Browse by category or search for your tool
   - Click on the integration you want to set up

3. **Authorize Access**:
   - Click "Connect" and you'll be redirected to the service
   - Sign in and authorize Digame to access your data
   - Choose what data to share (if options are available)

4. **Configure Settings**:
   - Select which projects, calendars, or data to sync
   - Set sync frequency and preferences
   - Test the connection to ensure it's working

5. **Verify Data Flow**:
   - Check that data is appearing in your dashboard
   - Allow 15-30 minutes for initial sync
   - Contact support if you encounter issues

### Q: What data does each integration access?
**A:** We follow the principle of minimal data access:

**Calendar Integrations**:
- **Read**: Event titles, times, attendees, locations
- **No Access**: Event content, private details, personal calendars (unless selected)

**Email Integrations**:
- **Read**: Email metadata (sender, recipient, timestamp, subject)
- **No Access**: Email content, attachments, private emails

**Project Management**:
- **Read**: Task titles, status, assignments, due dates, project structure
- **No Access**: Task descriptions, comments, private projects (unless selected)

**Communication Tools**:
- **Read**: Message metadata, channel participation, response times
- **No Access**: Message content, private conversations, personal DMs

**Always**:
- **Encrypted**: All data is encrypted in transit and at rest
- **Minimal**: Only necessary data for productivity insights
- **Controlled**: You choose what to share and can revoke access anytime

### Q: Can I disconnect an integration?
**A:** Yes, you can disconnect integrations at any time:

1. **Go to Settings → Integrations**
2. **Find the integration** you want to disconnect
3. **Click "Disconnect"** or the settings icon
4. **Confirm disconnection** in the popup dialog

**What Happens**:
- **Data Sync Stops**: No new data will be imported
- **Historical Data**: Previously imported data remains (unless you choose to delete it)
- **Insights**: Analytics based on that integration will no longer update
- **Re-connection**: You can reconnect the same integration later

**Data Retention Options**:
- **Keep Historical Data**: Maintain past insights and trends
- **Delete Integration Data**: Remove all data from that integration
- **Archive**: Keep data but exclude from active analytics

### Q: Why isn't my integration syncing data?
**A:** Common sync issues and solutions:

**Check Connection Status**:
- Go to Settings → Integrations
- Look for error indicators or "Disconnected" status
- Try disconnecting and reconnecting the integration

**Common Issues**:
- **Expired Tokens**: Re-authorize the integration
- **Permission Changes**: Ensure Digame still has necessary permissions
- **API Limits**: Some services have rate limits that may cause delays
- **Network Issues**: Check your internet connection

**Troubleshooting Steps**:
1. **Refresh the page** and check again
2. **Disconnect and reconnect** the integration
3. **Check service status** - the external service may be down
4. **Review permissions** in the external service
5. **Contact support** if issues persist

**Data Sync Timing**:
- **Real-time**: Some integrations sync immediately
- **Periodic**: Others sync every 15-30 minutes
- **Daily**: Some data is processed once daily
- **Manual**: You can trigger manual sync for most integrations

### Q: Why is the app running slowly?
**A:** Performance issues can have several causes:

**Common Causes**:
- **Large Data Sets**: Extensive historical data can slow loading
- **Multiple Integrations**: Many active integrations increase processing
- **Browser Issues**: Outdated browser or too many open tabs
- **Network Connectivity**: Slow or unstable internet connection

**Troubleshooting Steps**:
1. **Clear Browser Cache**: Clear cache and cookies for Digame
2. **Update Browser**: Ensure you're using the latest browser version
3. **Close Other Tabs**: Reduce browser memory usage
4. **Check Network**: Test internet speed and stability
5. **Disable Extensions**: Temporarily disable browser extensions
6. **Try Incognito Mode**: Test in private browsing mode

**Performance Optimization**:
- **Reduce Time Ranges**: Use shorter time periods for analytics
- **Limit Widgets**: Remove unused dashboard widgets
- **Optimize Integrations**: Disable unused integrations
- **Use Mobile App**: Mobile app may perform better for basic tasks

**When to Contact Support**:
- Performance issues persist after troubleshooting
- Specific error messages appear
- Issues affect multiple users in your organization
- Performance degraded suddenly without changes

### Q: I'm getting authentication errors. What should I do?
**A:** Authentication errors can usually be resolved quickly:

**Common Error Types**:
- **"Invalid credentials"**: Username/password incorrect
- **"Session expired"**: Login session has timed out
- **"Account locked"**: Too many failed login attempts
- **"SSO error"**: Single sign-on configuration issue

**Immediate Solutions**:
1. **Check Credentials**: Verify username and password are correct
2. **Clear Browser Data**: Clear cookies and cache
3. **Try Incognito Mode**: Test in private browsing window
4. **Reset Password**: Use "Forgot Password" if needed
5. **Check Caps Lock**: Ensure caps lock is not enabled

**SSO-Specific Issues**:
- **Contact IT Admin**: SSO issues often require admin assistance

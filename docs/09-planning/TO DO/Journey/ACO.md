The Digame Digital Professional Twin Platform provides comprehensive access control through a tiered subscription model designed to serve individual professionals, teams, and enterprises. This document outlines feature access, pricing, and special provisions for founding members and platform ownership.

- **All User Features**: Full access to every feature available across all tiers
